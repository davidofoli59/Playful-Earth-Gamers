Understanding Game Odds

Odds show the probability of an outcome and how much you’ll win if it happens. The lower the odds, the higher the chance. The higher the odds, the bigger the potential reward.

1.50High chance (Favourites)
2.50Medium chance
5.00Low chance (High reward)

Staking Options Per Player

Multiple ways to bet on a single player

Win the Match
Player wins the entire game/challenge.
Over/Under
Player’s total score, kills, or points.
First to X Kills
Player reaches a specific number first.
Exact Result
Exact score, time, or special event.
Combo Bet
Combine multiple options for higher odds.
