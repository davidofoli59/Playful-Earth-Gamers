Privacy & Data Governance

Compliant with global privacy standards including GDPR, NDPR, and California CCPA guidelines.

1. Zero Sale of Player Information

Playful Earth Gamers does not sell, rent, or lease player profiles, gaming behavior, or transaction histories to marketing third parties or advertising brokers. Your gaming telemetry belongs solely to your competitive identity.

2. Tokenized Payment Isolation

Primary card numbers and banking credentials never touch our direct application servers. All payment processing executes through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified partners (Paystack, Visa, Mastercard) via encrypted, one-time tokenization tunnels.

3. Competitive Telemetry & Verification

Telemetry captured during official tournament matches (such as ping latency, client session hashes, and match timestamps) is retained for 90 days solely to facilitate fair play dispute arbitration, after which logs are securely archived or purged.

4. Right to Erasure & Data Export

Every registered member maintains sovereign rights to request an exported archive of their match records, virtual card balance receipts, and profile history, or submit an immutable account deletion request through the Concierge Desk.
